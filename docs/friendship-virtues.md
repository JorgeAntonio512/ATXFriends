# Friendship Virtues (Simpatico v1 archive)

Verbatim copy of the 18 virtues (title + description) from the original Simpatico questionnaire, preserved before the v2 rewrite. May be reused as community-guidelines copy.

## Shared Interests & Hobbies

**Joint activities** — Doing things you both enjoy to build fun memories.

**Shared learning** — Trying new skills or exploring fresh places together.

**Quality time** — Dedicating focused, uninterrupted blocks of clock time to connect.

## Shared Values & Trust

**Life alignment** — Sharing similar views on big goals like money or family.

**Reliability** — Keeping your promises so your friend can count on you.

**Vulnerability** — Sharing your deepest fears knowing your friend will care for them.

## Clear Communication

**Active listening** — Hear your friend out without planning your reply.

**Honesty** — Share your true feelings in a kind way.

**Openness** — Talk about hard things instead of hiding them.

## Mutual Respect

**Boundaries** — Give each other space to be yourselves.

**Appreciation** — Thank your friend for small things.

**Equality** — Make big choices together as a team.

## Emotional Support

**Empathy** — Try to feel what your friend feels.

**Safety** — Be a safe place when life is hard.

**Encouragement** — Cheer for your friend's personal goals.

## Conflict Resolution

**Fair fighting** — Attack the problem, not the person.

**Forgiveness** — Let go of past mistakes after you talk them through.

**Compromise** — Meet in the middle when you disagree.
